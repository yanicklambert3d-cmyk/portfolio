ABOUT ME

YANICK LAMBERT

Designer / UX Researcher

Game Developer / Artist

lamberttrindade@hotmail.com



Hi, I’m Yanick. I’m a multidisciplinary designer, UX researcher, game developer, and artist with an academic and professional background spanning design, games, and user experience. My experience includes product design in marketing and advertising, game design and art in the video game industry, and UX research and development in lab and research institute environments. Over the last 10 years, user experience has become an increasingly central part of my work, with a strong focus on UX research, human-computer interaction, and understanding people’s needs, behaviours, and experiences. I apply these insights to the design of products, services, games, and other interactive and emerging technologies. I’m driven by curiosity and always looking for new ways to combine research, design, and technology to create meaningful and engaging experiences.
