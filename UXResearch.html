PROFESSIONAL WORK

My UX research focuses on understanding users across interactive experiences, including games and virtual reality. At ErgoUX, I conducted qualitative and quantitative studies addressing usability, user engagement, emotional response, and facial-expression analysis. More recently, at the Interactive Technologies Institute, I collaborated on HCI research exploring connections with the marine environment, alongside Carnegie Mellon University’s Center for Transformational Play, eGamesLab, Loga Culture, and the Natural History Museum of Funchal. Close collaboration with museum experts, followed by user testing, enabled us to identify stakeholder needs and translate them into a focused VR experience informed by research evidence.
